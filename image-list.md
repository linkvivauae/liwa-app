## Summary for the designer
- **19 unique photos** need creative treatment (some reused 2–3 times across screens as noted above).
- **1 logo** stays as-is (official brand asset).
- Photos that appear in *both* a small tile/thumbnail **and** a large banner (marked "Square-ish tile **and** wide banner" or similar above) need the subject positioned so it survives both a tighter square-ish crop and a wider landscape crop without losing the focal point — center-weighted compositions work best.
- Recommended minimum source resolution: **1920×1080** (current stock is all rights-cleared Unsplash + real Liwa Festival campaign photography at this resolution or higher).
